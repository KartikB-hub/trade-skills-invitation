Why this is for you

Try before you train. Handle real tools and complete a real task before committing to an apprenticeship or course.
Meet the person doing the job. Every session is led by a working tradesperson who can explain the work honestly.
Leave with a next step. Get a personalised recommendation for the pathway that best fits your interests.

What a session looks like

1
Welcome: A working tradesperson explains what the job really looks like day to day.
2
Safety and tools: Get introduced to the tools, components and safety basics.
3
Hands-on challenge: Complete a real, achievable task in 40 minutes.
4
Career clinic: Hear about entry routes, certifications, pay realities and demand.
5
Next-step debrief: Leave with a suggested pathway matched to your interests.
